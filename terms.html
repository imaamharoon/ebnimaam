Orders and payment

Orders are submitted after the customer provides accurate contact and delivery details. Payments are processed through Paystack. An order is considered paid only after the store's server verifies the payment with Paystack.

Pricing

Product prices are shown in Nigerian naira (NGN). Delivery charges are calculated according to the destination entered at checkout. Any applicable promotion is subject to its stated conditions.

Delivery

Delivery availability, fees and estimated delivery times should be confirmed with the store before an order is fulfilled. Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct.

Returns and exchanges

The store's final return, exchange, cancellation and refund policy must be supplied by the store owner and displayed here before accepting live customer orders.

Privacy

Customer information is used to process orders, arrange delivery and provide order support. Do not submit payment card details anywhere other than the secure Paystack checkout.
