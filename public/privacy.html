Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Applies to ebnimaam website, customer accounts and orders

This policy explains how ebnimaam (“we”, “us” or “the store”) handles personal information when you browse our website, create an account, contact us or place an order. We aim to handle personal data fairly, securely and only for clear business purposes, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable requirements.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

The store operating under the name ebnimaam is responsible for the personal information described here. For privacy questions or requests, contact ebnimaam.com@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 814 567 2755. We do not publish a business street address here because a verified public address has not been supplied.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, verification status and account/session identifiers.
  • Order and delivery information: items ordered, quantities, order reference, payment status, recipient name, phone number, delivery address, fulfilment updates and support messages.
  • Payment information: payment reference, amount, currency, channel and confirmation details returned by Paystack. Card and bank payment credentials are entered with Paystack; ebnimaam does not ask you to send card PINs, passwords or one-time banking codes to the store.
  • Technical information: essential session/cookie data, device/browser information and security or error records needed to operate and protect the website.
  • Messages you send us: information you choose to include when contacting customer support.

3. Why we use information and our lawful bases

  • To create and secure your account, authenticate you and provide account features — to perform the customer-account service you request and for legitimate security interests.
  • To calculate, receive and verify payment for orders, prevent fraud and provide receipts and order updates — to perform the purchase contract and meet legal/accounting obligations.
  • To arrange delivery or pickup, respond to questions and manage returns or refunds — to perform the purchase contract and handle customer requests.
  • To maintain the website, troubleshoot errors and protect it from abuse — for legitimate operational and security interests.
  • To send optional marketing or newsletter messages — only where you have chosen to receive them; you can withdraw that choice at any time. The website does not currently treat a placeholder newsletter form as a subscription service.

4. When information is shared

We share only the information needed with service providers that help operate the store: Vercel for website hosting and delivery, Neon for database and account authentication, Paystack for payment processing, and delivery or fulfilment partners where needed to complete your order. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect customers or the store, or as part of a lawful business reorganisation. We do not publish customer order details or make customer account information publicly searchable.

5. Payment security

Payments are handled through Paystack's secure payment service. The store receives transaction references and verification information so it can reconcile the payment with your order. Do not send card PINs, passwords, OTPs or full card details to us by email, WhatsApp or a website form.

6. International processing

Our hosting, database, authentication and payment providers may process or store information in locations outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we use service providers and contractual or other safeguards intended to protect personal information as required by applicable data-protection law. Contact us for further information about relevant safeguards.

7. How long we keep information

We keep account and contact information while your account is active and for a reasonable period needed to provide the service. Order, payment, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute records may be retained for as long as required by applicable law or reasonably needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Security logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary. When information is no longer required, we take steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to lawful retention and backup cycles.

8. Cookies, local storage and app functions

We use essential session cookies for account sign-in and security. The shopping bag may be stored locally in your browser so it remains available as you move around the shop. The installable web app may cache essential website resources for reliability. You can clear browser storage or cookies, but doing so may sign you out or remove a locally saved cart. We do not currently claim to run an advertising-tracking or analytics programme on this storefront.

9. Your choices and data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of your identity, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data and request access to it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing where the law permits;
  • receive a copy of information you provided in a commonly used format where applicable;
  • withdraw consent for optional marketing, without affecting processing already lawfully carried out; and
  • raise a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Send requests to ebnimaam.com@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the relevant account. Some order/payment records may need to be retained where law requires it. Account deletion controls are not represented as available until the secure deletion process is implemented and verified.

10. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, service-provider security features and operational safeguards intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password, keep your sign-in details private and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

11. Children

The store is intended for people who can lawfully enter a purchase contract. A parent or guardian should contact us if a child has submitted personal information without appropriate authorisation.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.
