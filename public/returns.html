Returns, exchanges & refunds

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Nigeria orders only

We want you to be comfortable shopping with ebnimaam. This policy explains how to request a return, exchange, cancellation or refund. It does not remove or limit any consumer rights you have under Nigerian law. Where a legal right gives you a better remedy, that right continues to apply.

1. Change-of-mind returns

You may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for an item that is unused, unworn, unwashed, in its original condition and returned with its original packaging and accessories. Please keep proof of purchase and the order reference. We will review the request and explain the return steps before you send anything back.

For a change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for reasonable return delivery costs unless applicable law requires otherwise or we agree in writing to cover them. The original delivery charge is not automatically refundable for a change of mind where the law permits it to be excluded.

2. Damaged, faulty, incomplete or incorrect items

Please contact us within 7 calendar days after delivery when an item arrives damaged, faulty, incomplete or different from what you ordered. Include your order reference, a description and clear photographs where possible. We will assess the issue and arrange an appropriate repair, replacement or refund in line with applicable law. Where the issue is our error or the goods arrived damaged/faulty, we will cover reasonable return or collection costs.

3. How to request a return

  1. Email ebnimaam.com@gmail.com with the subject “Return or refund request”, or contact us on WhatsApp +234 814 567 2755.
  2. Provide your order reference, the email or phone number used for the order, the item(s), and the reason for the request. For damage or an incorrect item, attach photographs where possible.
  3. Wait for our instructions and return authorisation before sending an item. We will confirm the return destination and available method directly; do not send goods to a payment-provider or hosting address.
  4. Pack the item securely and keep your dispatch receipt or tracking details.

4. Inspection and outcome

When the item is received, we will check its condition and confirm whether the request is accepted. We will not unreasonably delay that review. If the return is accepted, the available remedy will be a replacement, exchange, repair or refund as appropriate to the issue and applicable law. If a return is declined, we will explain why and discuss return of the item to you.

5. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds for Paystack-paid orders are normally sent through the original payment route where the provider supports it. We will initiate the refund after approval and any required inspection. The time for the credit to appear depends on Paystack, the card issuer, bank or payment channel; we cannot promise a bank posting date. We will provide the relevant order/payment reference and any confirmation available to us.

6. Order cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. If the order has not been dispatched, we will try to stop fulfilment and arrange a refund for any amount paid where appropriate. Once an order has been dispatched or delivered, the returns process above may apply. We cannot guarantee cancellation after fulfilment has begun.

7. Items that may not be returnable

Some goods may be excluded from change-of-mind returns where an exclusion is lawful and was clearly disclosed before purchase (for example, genuinely personalised goods or certain sealed hygiene-sensitive products after the seal is broken). Such exclusions do not remove remedies for faulty, damaged, misdescribed or incorrectly supplied goods, or other rights that cannot legally be excluded.

8. Contact and escalation

Contact us first so we have an opportunity to resolve your issue. Email ebnimaam.com@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 814 567 2755. If we cannot resolve a consumer complaint, you may seek guidance or lodge a complaint with the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC).
