Returns, exchanges & refunds

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Nigeria orders only

We want you to be comfortable shopping with ebnimaam. This policy explains how to request a return, exchange, cancellation or refund. It does not remove or limit any consumer rights you have under Nigerian law. Where a legal right gives you a better remedy, that right continues to apply.

1. Your right to return goods

Under section 122 of Nigeria’s Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018, consumers have a right to return goods (except in limited circumstances) when the goods remain in good condition and are returned within a reasonable time. A return request is assessed against the product, its condition, the time since delivery and the circumstances. Where a return is accepted under this statutory right, the applicable refund remedy will be provided. We will not use a short store-imposed deadline to remove a right that the law gives you.

Please keep the item, its accessories and packaging in good condition while you contact us. Reasonable return arrangements and any cost allocation will be explained before you send the item. Any term about return costs or exclusions is subject to applicable consumer law.

2. Damaged, faulty, incomplete or incorrect items

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible when an item arrives damaged, faulty, incomplete or different from what you ordered. Include your order reference, a description and clear photographs where possible. We will assess the issue and provide the remedy required by applicable law, which may include repair, replacement or refund. This request guidance does not create a cut-off or remove any consumer right or remedy you have under law.

3. How to request a return

  1. Email ebnimaam.com@gmail.com with the subject “Return or refund request”, or contact us on WhatsApp +234 814 567 2755.
  2. Provide your order reference, the email or phone number used for the order, the item(s), and the reason for the request. For damage or an incorrect item, attach photographs where possible.
  3. Wait for our instructions and return authorisation before sending an item. We will confirm the return destination and available method directly; do not send goods to a payment-provider or hosting address.
  4. Pack the item securely and keep your dispatch receipt or tracking details.

4. Inspection and outcome

When the item is received, we will review its condition and the circumstances and explain the outcome. We will handle the request without unreasonable delay. Any refusal will include the reason and will not purport to override a statutory consumer right. Where the law requires a refund, the appropriate refund will be arranged.

5. Refund method and timing

For a Paystack-paid order, we will contact you to confirm the approved refund amount and the available refund method. Refund processing is handled by the store through the payment provider or other lawful agreed method; the time for funds to appear can depend on Paystack, the issuing bank and payment channel. We will share the relevant order/payment reference and any available confirmation. The website does not currently claim that refunds are automatically processed by an integrated refund API.

6. Order cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. If the order has not been dispatched, we will try to stop fulfilment and arrange a refund for any amount paid where appropriate. Once an order has been dispatched or delivered, the returns process above may apply. We cannot guarantee cancellation after fulfilment has begun.

7. Items that may not be returnable

Limited statutory exceptions may apply to particular goods or circumstances. Any exception relied on by the store must be lawful, relevant to the item and circumstances, and disclosed clearly before purchase where required. No exception in this policy removes a remedy for faulty, unsafe, damaged, misdescribed or incorrectly supplied goods where the law provides a remedy.

8. Contact and escalation

Contact us first so we have an opportunity to resolve your issue. Email ebnimaam.com@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 814 567 2755. If we cannot resolve a consumer complaint, you may seek guidance or lodge a complaint with the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC).
